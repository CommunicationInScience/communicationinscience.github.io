Communication in Science (CIS) assists health workers and biomedical scientists worldwide who wish to publish their research in English-language journals.

CIS was founded by Jonathan Polonsky and offers workshops, editing services, and online tutorials to help scientists communicate their work effectively.

For enquiries, please contact us at [jonathan@communicationinscience.com](mailto:jonathan@communicationinscience.com).
