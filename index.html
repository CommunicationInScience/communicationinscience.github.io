Communication in Science (CIS) addresses the needs of health workers and biomedical scientists all over the world who wish to publish in English-language journals.

Workshops

Helping scientists to communicate more effectively, and to present the results of their research in a way that will best convince editors, reviewers and readers that their work is important and reliable.

Editing Services

Editing of reports, monographs and other documents, as well as draft manuscripts before submission to English-language biomedical journals.

Online Tutorials

Tuition in writing different elements of a scientific paper.
