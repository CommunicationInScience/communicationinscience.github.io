## What Our Editing Covers

Our editors are trained in biomedical and public health writing. When we review your manuscript, we look at both language and structure:

<div class="cis-two-col">
<div>

**Language & Style**
- Grammar, spelling, and punctuation
- Sentence clarity and concision
- Scientific vocabulary and terminology
- Consistency of tense and voice

</div>
<div>

**Structure & Argument**
- Logical flow within and between sections
- Use of the IMRaD framework
- Coherence of the argument
- Alignment with target journal style

</div>
</div>

## Our Process

<ol class="cis-steps">
  <li><strong>Submit your manuscript</strong> Send us your document along with the target journal (if known) and your deadline.</li>
  <li><strong>We review and edit</strong> Your manuscript is returned with tracked changes and explanatory comments throughout.</li>
  <li><strong>Discuss if needed</strong> We are happy to clarify any edits and answer questions about the changes made.</li>
  <li><strong>Final check</strong> Once your revisions are complete, we can perform a final light review before submission.</li>
</ol>
