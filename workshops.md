Our workshops have been delivered at research institutes, schools of public health, universities, ministries of health, and NGOs across Africa, Asia, and Europe. We work with:

<ul class="cis-check-list">
  <li><i class="fa fa-check-circle"></i> Early-career researchers preparing their first publications</li>
  <li><i class="fa fa-check-circle"></i> Established scientists seeking to improve their success rate with high-impact journals</li>
  <li><i class="fa fa-check-circle"></i> Research teams looking for a shared framework for writing and reviewing manuscripts</li>

## What We Cover

<div class="cis-two-col">
<div>

### Writing a Scientific Manuscript
- The IMRaD structure: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion
- Writing compelling titles and abstracts
- Logical flow and argument construction
- Adapting your manuscript for different journals

### The Publishing Process
- Choosing the right journal
- Navigating the submission process
- Responding to peer reviewers and editors
- Understanding copyright and open access

</div>
<div>

### Communicating to Broader Audiences
- Writing for policymakers and non-specialists
- Science communication in low-resource settings
- Structuring reports and policy briefs

### Presenting Your Research
- Designing effective posters and slides
- Oral presentations at conferences
- Structuring and delivering a scientific argument

</div>
</div>
