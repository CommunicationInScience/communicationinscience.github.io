## What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond as efficiently as possible, please mention:

- **For editing:** the document type, approximate word count, target journal (if known), and your deadline
- **For workshops:** the size of your group, experience level, preferred topic areas, and format (in-person or online)
- **For tutorials:** the number of users and your preferred access arrangement

We look forward to hearing from you.
