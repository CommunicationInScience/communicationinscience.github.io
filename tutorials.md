<div class="cis-page-lead">
  Our online tutorials allow researchers to study the craft of scientific writing at their own pace, revisiting material as often as needed. Each module focuses on a specific element of a scientific paper or the publishing process.
</div>

## Available Modules

<div class="cis-module-list">

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-file-text-o"></i> &nbsp;Writing a Scientific Paper: An Overview</h3>
An introduction to the IMRaD structure and the conventions of biomedical writing. Suitable for researchers preparing their first manuscript or looking for a structured refresher.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-arrow-right"></i> &nbsp;How to Write an Introduction</h3>
Learn to open your paper effectively: setting context, identifying the knowledge gap, and stating your research aims clearly and concisely.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-cogs"></i> &nbsp;Describing Your Methods</h3>
A guide to writing a Methods section that is transparent, reproducible, and in line with reporting standards (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, and others).
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-bar-chart"></i> &nbsp;Reporting Your Results</h3>
How to present findings clearly and objectively, use tables and figures effectively, and avoid common errors in reporting statistics.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-comments"></i> &nbsp;Writing the Discussion</h3>
Structure your interpretation of results, situate your findings in the literature, acknowledge limitations honestly, and draw meaningful conclusions.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-align-left"></i> &nbsp;Crafting a Compelling Abstract</h3>
Write structured and unstructured abstracts that accurately represent your work and attract the attention of editors and readers.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-search"></i> &nbsp;Choosing the Right Journal</h3>
How to identify the most appropriate journal for your manuscript, understand scope and impact, and navigate submission requirements.
</div>

<div class="greyblock">
<h3><i class="fa fa-reply"></i> &nbsp;Responding to Reviewers</h3>
Strategies for addressing peer reviewer comments constructively and professionally, to maximise your chances of acceptance at revision stage.
</div>

</div>
